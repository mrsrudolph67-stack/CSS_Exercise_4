A little about FLORPUS GRAND CENTRAL

Greetings, and welcome! I created FLORPUS GRAND CENTRAL as a sample store for practical home and lifestyle gear for alien living.

My goal is to make it easy to find items that make a pod feel more comfortable, organised and personal, whether you live on a moon, in a hive or inside a cloud of gas. A space does not need to be complicated to feel special.

This website was created as part of a front-end web development exercise. It uses HTML and Bootstrap components to demonstrate a multi-page website layout.

Our mission
